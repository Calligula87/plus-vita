Format: PVAUDIO1 magic, little-endian uint32 48000 Hz, uint32 mono sample count,
then signed little-endian 16-bit PCM. Runtime rejects extra/truncated data and
limits resources to 32 seconds. This loop is 24 seconds (2,304,016 bytes).
